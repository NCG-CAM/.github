## Four Beats

1. **Name the mold.** Give the NCG CAM mold job a short title before you leave leftover stock.
2. **Pin the live faces.** Keep current surfaces on NCG CAM surface machining and leave unused patches aside.
3. **Read the speed paths.** Open NCG CAM high speed toolpaths before you move a cut that already has a rest.
4. **Check the rest.** Read NCG CAM rest finishing before you archive a cavity.
5. **Keep the pair.** Leave speed paths and rest finishing on the same NCG CAM mold job.
6. **Leave the cavity ready.** Keep leftover stock aligned with NCG CAM surface machining.
7. **Close the loop.** Clear finished faces so the next NCG CAM visit does not start with a search.

## First Questions

**Does NCG CAM replace every other program?**
No. NCG CAM sits beside the machine list, browser, and files you already use.

**Can two people share one computer?**
Yes. Each person keeps a separate NCG CAM profile.

**What should a new user try first?**
One small NCG CAM surface today, then the same rest finishing tomorrow.

**Do high speed toolpaths leave the mold job?**
No. NCG CAM high speed toolpaths stay with NCG CAM rest finishing on that cavity.

**Is NCG CAM only for a finished mold?**
No. NCG CAM can hold a single face while the rest of the toolroom stays off the board.

**Does leftover stock need a second job?**
No. NCG CAM keeps surface machining and rest finishing on this mold job.

## Who Sits Down at NCG CAM

## Field Note

NCG CAM is easier to trust when the mold job stays smaller than the folder it came from. A named cavity shows what is active, the surface pins show what you will cut today, and the closing note tells the next person which rest pass changed.

The same loop fits a single face, a rest pass, or a toolroom shared by two people. Open the last NCG CAM mold job, finish one high speed toolpath, and leave fewer leftover-stock files than you found.

A review should not have to guess which surface belongs to the open rest pass. Keep that pair together inside NCG CAM.

## One Real Pass

Pick a cavity that already has a name. Open it in NCG CAM, keep only those faces on the mold job, and finish one rest finishing pass you can see.

If the pass feels crowded, the set is too wide. Drop anything that belongs to a different mold and leave it out of this NCG CAM mold job.
